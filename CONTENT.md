# Website content

All the text currently on allingforddroneservices.co.uk, section by section, in the order it appears.

How to use this file:
- Edit the text directly on the `dev` branch and commit.
- Leave the headings and labels (in **bold**) as they are so each piece can be matched to its place on the page.
- Add new bullets, paragraphs or notes freely. Anything in a `> Note:` line is read as an instruction, not page text.
- Editing this file does not change the website. Tell Claude when it's ready and the changes will be reviewed and applied to the page.

## Privacy notice page

**Title:**
Privacy notice

**Date line:**
Last updated: November 2026

**Who I am:**
Allingford Drone Services is a trading name of Ewan Allen-Cottingham, a sole trader. I am responsible for the personal information described here. You can contact me at ewan@allingforddroneservices.co.uk.

**What I collect:**
When you send an enquiry, through the contact form, the drone requirements form or by email, I collect the details you give me. This is usually your name, organisation, email address, phone number and information about your project.

Both forms use Cloudflare Turnstile to check that submissions come from a real person. To do this Cloudflare processes technical information about your browser and connection, as described in the Turnstile Privacy Addendum (link).

The drone requirements form shows a map from OpenStreetMap and can look up postcodes using postcodes.io. Your browser contacts these services when the map loads or when you search for a postcode.

This website does not use cookies or analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes.

**How I use it:**
I use your details to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote, and carry out and invoice any work you ask me to do. I do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

**Legal basis:**
I process your information because it is needed to respond to your request before entering into a contract, to perform that contract, and for my legitimate interest in running my business.

**Where it is stored:**
Enquiries and project records are stored in my Google Workspace account. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages.

**How long I keep it:**
I keep enquiry and project information for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and any resulting work, and to meet my legal and accounting obligations.

**Your rights:**
You can ask to see, correct or delete the information I hold about you, or object to how I use it. Email me and I will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how I have handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
